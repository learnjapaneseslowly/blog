How to Use Notion to Learn Japanese

Short answer: keep vocabulary, grammar, reading, and listening in one Notion system. Scattered notes are the usual reason learners lose words. One system, checked daily, is enough.

What should the system include?

  • Daily habit tracker: 5–10 new words, 15 minutes of listening or reading, 3 sentences, and a review.
  • Vocabulary database: Word, Reading, Meaning, Example, Level (N5 / N4 / N3), Review date, and Known.
  • Grammar notes: one pattern, one example, and one sentence of your own.
  • Reading log: date, title, type, new words, and difficulty.
  • Listening log: date, what you listened to, minutes, and how much you understood.
  • Weekly review: what you learned, what was hard, and what to change next week.

How do I start?

Start with the free 7-day sample. It has a 7-day habit tracker and the 10 starter words from 10 Japanese Words Every Beginner Should Learn First. If the routine fits, get the full Japanese Learning Notebook for $12.

Yes. A free Notion account is enough for this template, on phone, tablet, and computer.
