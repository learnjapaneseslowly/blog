How to Build a Simple Notion System for Your Daily Habits

Short answer: keep three things in one place — a daily checklist, a simple log, and a weekly review. Most habit systems fail because they are too big. Start small, use it every day, and add to it only when the small version works.

What should the system include?

  • Daily habit tracker: 2–4 habits, checked off each day. No more than four at first.
  • Daily log: one line a day — what you did, and how it went.
  • Weekly review: what worked, what was hard, and one small change for next week.
  • Goal setting: one goal for the month, written in one sentence.

What if I miss a day?

Just start again the next day. Do not try to “catch up” by doing double. The system works because you return to it, not because you are perfect. A missed day is normal; a missed week is the problem — and a 15-minute routine makes a missed week less likely.
