How to Use AI to Learn Anything Faster in 15 Minutes a Day

Short answer: yes, AI can help you learn faster — but only if you use it to practise, not to get answers. Ask it to quiz you, explain a mistake, and give you examples. Do not ask it to do the work for you.

What should I do in those 15 minutes?

Split the time into three small blocks. Use AI as a patient tutor in each one.

BlockTimeDo this
Learn5 minAsk AI to explain one new idea in simple words, with one example.
Practise7 minAnswer 5 questions from AI. Try first, then check.
Review3 minAsk AI to explain your mistakes, then write the correction in your own words.

What should I ask AI?

  • Explain it simply: “Explain this like I am a beginner, with one example.”
  • Quiz me: “Ask me 5 questions, one at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.”
  • Check my work: “Here is my answer. What is wrong, and why?”
  • Give me examples: “Give me 3 everyday examples of this.”

What should I not ask AI to do?

Do not ask AI to write your homework, your essay, or your practice sentences for you. If AI does the thinking, you learn nothing. The rule is simple: you try first, AI checks second.

How do I know if AI is wrong?

AI can be wrong, especially about facts, dates, and grammar rules. Check anything important in a second source — a textbook, a dictionary, or a trusted website. If two sources disagree, trust the one you can verify.

How do I keep it as a daily habit?

Use the same time every day, and keep a short log: what you learned, what you got wrong, and what to practise tomorrow. One page for everything works best — see How to Build a Simple Notion System for Your Daily Habits.
