How to Start Listening to Japanese When You Understand Almost Nothing

Short answer: listen for the words you already know, not for everything. Seven minutes a day with one short clip, listened to twice, is enough to start. Understanding almost nothing at first is normal.

What should I listen to?

Pick something short and slow: a beginner dialogue, a short story read aloud, or a scene you have already seen with Japanese subtitles. The clip should be 2–5 minutes long. Long podcasts and fast TV shows can wait — right now, short and repeatable beats long and impressive.

What do I do in those 7 minutes?

Listen to the same clip twice, with a different job each time.

ListenTimeDo this
1st3–4 minJust listen. Do not pause, do not look anything up. Notice the words you recognise — even one or two counts.
2nd3–4 minListen again with the transcript or subtitles. Catch the words you missed, and write down 1–3 new ones.

Why listen to the same clip twice?

The first listen trains your ear: sounds, rhythm, and the words you know. The second listen connects those sounds to meaning. A new clip every day feels like progress, but the same clip twice teaches you more — and tomorrow you can pick a new one.

What if I understand almost nothing?

That is the starting point, not a problem. Count what you catch, not what you miss. If you recognised ありがとう (thank you) and 猫 (cat) in a clip, you listened well. Next week, the same kind of clip will give you a few more words. Progress in listening is quiet — it shows up as a word you suddenly hear without trying.

How do I keep track of it?

Keep a short listening log: date, what you listened to, how long, and the words you caught. One line a day is enough. Over weeks, the log shows you what is getting easier — and which words keep appearing, which are the ones worth learning next. Put new words from listening straight into your vocabulary list, with a review date — the schedule in How to Remember Japanese Words Without Forgetting Them works for these too.

Where does listening fit in my routine?

It is the 7-minute input block in How to Learn Japanese in 15 Minutes a Day: 5 minutes of new words, 7 minutes of listening or reading, 3 minutes of review.
