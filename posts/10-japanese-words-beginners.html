10 Japanese Words Every Beginner Should Learn First

Short answer: start with greetings, please, thank you, and everyday nouns. These 10 words appear in real conversations on day one, and each one teaches you a useful pattern.

How do I remember them?

Do not just read the list. Say each word out loud, write one sentence of your own, and review the list tomorrow. That is the 15-minute routine in How to Learn Japanese in 15 Minutes a Day.
