How to Learn Japanese in 15 Minutes a Day

Short answer: yes, 15 minutes a day is enough to start. The trick is not more time. It is the same small routine, every day: 5 new words, 15 minutes of listening or reading, and a short review.

What should I do in those 15 minutes?

Split the time into three small blocks. Do them in order, and stop when the timer ends.

BlockTimeDo this
New words5 minLearn 5–10 words. Write the word, reading, meaning, and one example sentence.
Input7 minListen to or read one short text. Do not try to understand everything.
Review3 minReview yesterday's words. Mark the ones you know.

Why does this work?

Japanese is a long game. A big study session once a week fades. A small daily habit builds vocabulary, listening, and reading at the same time — and it is easy to restart after a missed day.

What if I miss a day?

Just start again the next day. Do not double the time to “catch up.” A missed day is normal. A missed week is the problem, and a 15-minute routine makes a missed week less likely.
